Version: 1.0

# Capability Model

SHOPWISE is organized around business capabilities rather than technical services.

---

## C1 Product Discovery

Purpose

Help users discover relevant products.

Functions

- Search
- Filtering
- Semantic Search

---

## C2 Requirement Understanding

Purpose

Transform natural language into structured shopping requirements.

Functions

- Intent Detection
- Entity Extraction
- Preference Identification

---

## C3 Recommendation

Purpose

Recommend products matching customer goals.

Functions

- Candidate Generation
- Ranking
- Personalized Recommendation

---

## C4 Product Comparison

Purpose

Compare products from multiple perspectives.

Functions

- Specification Comparison
- Performance Comparison
- Price Comparison
- Trade-off Analysis

---

## C5 Decision Support

Purpose

Reduce purchase uncertainty.

Functions

- Explain reasoning
- Confidence score
- Alternative suggestions

---

## C6 Commerce Actions

Purpose

Execute retailer operations.

Functions

- Add to Cart
- Apply Coupon
- Checkout
- Reserve Inventory

---

## C7 Knowledge Assistance

Purpose

Provide product knowledge.

Functions

- Warranty
- Promotions
- Buying Guide
- FAQ

---

## C8 Decision Memory

Purpose

Remember previous customer decisions.

Functions

- Preferences
- Purchase History
- Previous Conversations

---

## C9 Dynamic UI

Purpose

Generate interactive shopping experiences.

Functions

- Product Cards
- Comparison Tables
- Charts
- Checkout Summary
