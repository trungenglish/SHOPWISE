# Discovery

Customer arrives on retailer website.

Needs are often vague.

Example

"I need a laptop for AI."

---

# Requirement Understanding

SHOPWISE asks contextual questions.

Examples

Budget

Preferred brands

Usage

Weight

Battery

Gaming

AI/ML

The objective is to transform vague intent into structured requirements.

---

# Recommendation

SHOPWISE generates personalized recommendations.

Every recommendation includes:

- Why
- Trade-offs
- Alternatives

---

# Comparison

Customers compare multiple products.

Comparison includes:

- Specifications
- Performance
- Price
- Warranty
- Value

---

# Decision Support

SHOPWISE explains:

Why Product A is better than Product B.

What compromises exist.

What future needs should be considered.
