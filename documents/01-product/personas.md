# Personas

Version: 1.0

---

# Overview

SHOPWISE serves multiple stakeholders within the retail ecosystem.

The primary user is the online shopper. Other personas benefit indirectly through operational improvements and business insights.

---

# Primary Persona

## Electronics Shopper

### Description

Consumers purchasing consumer electronics through online retail platforms.

### Goals

- Find suitable products efficiently.
- Understand technical specifications.
- Compare alternatives.
- Purchase with confidence.

### Pain Points

- Too many similar products.
- Difficult technical terminology.
- Scattered information.
- Uncertainty before purchase.

### AI Needs

- Requirement understanding.
- Personalized recommendations.
- Product comparison.
- Explainable reasoning.

---

# Secondary Persona

## Returning Customer

### Description

Customers who have previous shopping history.

### Goals

- Faster purchasing.
- Personalized suggestions.
- Access previous decisions.

### AI Needs

- Decision memory.
- Preference learning.
- Accessory recommendation.
- Upgrade recommendation.

---

# Retail Operations

### Description

Retail teams responsible for customer support.

### Goals

- Reduce repetitive inquiries.
- Increase conversion.
- Improve customer satisfaction.

### AI Needs

- Automated product Q&A.
- Warranty lookup.
- Promotion lookup.
- Inventory assistance.

---

# Retail Management

### Description

Business owners and managers.

### Goals

- Increase conversion.
- Increase Average Order Value.
- Reduce support costs.

### Success Metrics

- Conversion Rate
- Support Deflection
- Customer Satisfaction
- Average Order Value
