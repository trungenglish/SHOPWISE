# Domain Events

Version: 1.0

---

# ConversationStarted

Occurs when a customer begins a shopping session.

---

# RequirementsUpdated

Occurs when customer preferences change.

---

# ProductsRetrieved

Occurs after search results are returned.

---

# RecommendationGenerated

Occurs after recommendation engine completes.

---

# ComparisonRequested

Occurs when customers compare products.

---

# RecommendationAccepted

Occurs when customer chooses a recommendation.

---

# ProductAddedToCart

Occurs after successful cart operation.

---

# CouponApplied

Occurs after promotion validation.

---

# CheckoutStarted

Occurs before payment.

---

# CheckoutCompleted

Occurs after successful purchase.

---

# OrderCreated

Occurs when retailer confirms order.

---

# MemoryStored

Occurs when customer preferences are persisted.

---

# ConversationEnded

Occurs when the shopping session ends.
