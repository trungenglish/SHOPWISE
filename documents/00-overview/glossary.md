# Glossary

---

## AI Decision Engine

The core intelligence layer responsible for understanding customer intent, reasoning over product information, and generating recommendations.

---

## Retail Connector

An adapter that integrates SHOPWISE with retailer APIs.

---

## Tool

A callable capability that allows the AI to retrieve information or execute business actions.

Examples:

- Search Products
- Compare Products
- Add to Cart
- Checkout

---

## Tool Calling

The process where an AI agent invokes external tools instead of relying solely on language generation.

---

## Commerce Action

A business operation executed on behalf of the customer.

Examples:

- Add to Cart
- Apply Coupon
- Checkout

---

## Product Discovery

The stage where customers explore products before making a purchasing decision.

---

## Decision Support

The process of helping customers understand trade-offs and make informed purchasing decisions.

---

## Recommendation

A personalized product suggestion generated through AI reasoning.

---

## Reasoning

The transparent explanation describing why a recommendation is appropriate.

---

## Dynamic UI

A UI rendered from structured schemas generated by the AI instead of fixed page layouts.

---

## AG-UI

A protocol that enables AI agents to generate interactive UI schemas rendered by frontend applications.

---

## Commerce Protocol

A standardized interface that allows AI agents to execute commerce actions independent of retailer implementations.

---

## Retail Provider

An implementation of the Commerce Protocol for a specific retailer such as Phong Vũ or Shopee.

---

## Capability

A high-level business function delivered by the system.

Examples:

- Product Recommendation
- Product Comparison
- Decision Support
- Commerce Actions

Capabilities are decomposed into workflows, tools, APIs, and UI components.
