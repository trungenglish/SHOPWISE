# Problem Statement

Version: 1.0

---

## Background

Modern e-commerce platforms provide thousands of products but very limited decision support.

Customers often leave the website to search for reviews, comparisons, or buying advice from external sources such as Google, Reddit, or YouTube.

This behavior increases drop-off rates and decreases conversion.

---

## Business Problem

Online shoppers lack real-time guidance during product discovery and decision-making.

As a result:

- product discovery becomes overwhelming
- customers lose confidence
- purchase decisions are delayed
- conversion rates decrease

---

## Root Causes

### Information Overload

Customers face hundreds of similar products with insufficient contextual guidance.

### Fragmented Information

Specifications, reviews, promotions, warranty information, and buying advice are scattered across multiple sources.

### Lack of Personalization

Traditional search and filtering cannot understand customer goals.

### Limited Human Assistance

Sales consultants are unavailable during most online shopping sessions.

## Target Problem

---

## Business Objectives

Increase conversion rate

Reduce product discovery drop-off

Improve shopping experience

Reduce repetitive customer support requests

Increase customer confidence

---

## Non-goals

SHOPWISE is not intended to:

- replace the retailer's e-commerce platform
- replace payment infrastructure
- replace inventory management
- replace ERP systems
- replace human experts in complex cases

Instead, SHOPWISE augments existing retail systems with AI-powered decision intelligence.
